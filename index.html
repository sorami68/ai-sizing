    How the numbers are estimated
    • Weights: parameters × bytes (BF16 2, FP8 1, INT4 0.5). Runtime overhead: 5% of weights plus 2.5 GB per GPU for CUDA context, graphs and activations.
    • KV cache per token: 2 × layers × KV heads × head dim × KV bytes, reserved for the full prompt + output window for every concurrent user.
    • Decode is treated as memory-bandwidth bound at 70% of peak. Each step reads the weights once (for MoE, the share of experts a batch touches) plus every user's KV cache.
    • Prefill (time to first token) uses 45% of dense peak TFLOPS, FP8 where the weights are FP8/INT4 and the GPU supports it. Queueing is not modeled.
    • Tensor parallel penalty: 8% over NVLink, 25% over PCIe, another 30% when one replica spans nodes.
    • Fine-tuning assumes FSDP / ZeRO-3 sharding with activation checkpointing. Full tuning keeps 16 bytes per parameter (BF16 weights and gradients, FP32 master copy and Adam states). LoRA keeps frozen weights at the chosen precision plus adapters at 1% of parameters. Training throughput assumes 40% MFU.
    • GPU specs are approximate public dense figures (no sparsity) and can differ by SKU, clocks and form factor. Confirm against vendor datasheets and your server's supported configuration before ordering.
